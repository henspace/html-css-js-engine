Henspace games collection

Hi! These games are a collection of open source test games created using my mini html-css-js-engine or HCJE. They're all very simple, more like game jams, but allow me to test different features of the engine.

  • Bridge over Jupiter - get your astronauts over the meteors.

Privacy

No personal data is collected by the games and no cookies are used. Some games will use your browser's local storage to save scores or other game data, but no personal data is collected by the games themselves; however, the test games are hosted on GitHub, so you should check their privacy policy to review any data they may collect.
