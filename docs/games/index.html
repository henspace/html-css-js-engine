HCJE Sample Games

Welcome to the html-css-js-engine(HCJE) sample games index. Here you will find a selection of games developed to test the functionality of the HCJE. Most are simple little games to help you waste a few valuable minutes of your day.

Games index

  • Bridge over Jupiter - the first every game using the HCJE.
  • I8Everything - coming soon, so watch this space. Soon is a very relative term though.

Privacy

  • No personal data is collected by the games and no cookies are used. I have neither the time nor inclination to look at your data. 😀
  • Your browser's local storage is used to save game data such as your best score, but there is no online storage of your information.
  • Hosting of the games is via GitHub, and so depending on whether you have logged in to GitHub, some data may be collected by the platform; refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement for more information.

Why have you written these?

Some games have been written specifically to test new features of the HCJE. Others, to be honest, have been written because I had a bit of spare time and I like writing quick little games.
